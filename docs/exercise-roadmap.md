# Further exercise directions

Reviewed October 26, 2026. This is a product research note, not a treatment plan. Study protocols, displays, participants and supervision differ from Mangekyo. A related laboratory task does not validate this app or establish a benefit for a particular person. No training dose or patching schedule is recommended here.

## Now available: touch coordination

**Tap targets** practices visually guided pointing. It records hits, attempt latency, distance from the target center, target size and hit tolerance. A miss retains the target and earned points. The hit area never falls below 44 CSS pixels. Five consecutive centered hits within the displayed deadline earn a level. A separate controller tunes target size, tolerance, pace and jump distance toward a provisional success band, independently from scheduling and earned levels. Optional confirmation/level sounds and a goal meter make accomplishments explicit.

**Follow the target** practices continuous cursor control. A handle 56 CSS pixels below the cursor keeps the finger away from the visual target. Dragging starts active timing; releasing, losing contact, pausing, interruption or geometry changes stop it. Recovery requires confirmation and a fresh grab. Reports contain time within the halo, time-weighted mean distance, and best continuous time on target. Ten seconds on target earn a level; a one-second slip is tolerated without receiving on-target credit. Separate adaptation starts with quick calibration, then tunes dot size, halo and phase-continuous speed near recent performance. A halfway cue at five seconds and a level-up sound are optional. Earned points and levels survive interruptions and easier settings. [Balancing rules and sources](adaptive-balancing.md).

Adult research has documented reach/grasp coordination differences in amblyopia. That provides a reason to explore coordination tasks; it does not establish the treatment efficacy of these two phone activities. [Grant and Conway, 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38032338/).

## Now available: crowded symbols

The seventh exercise asks for the direction of the central C-like opening among two nearby flankers. The symbol diameter stays fixed while the gap adapts independently after five-answer blocks. Custom sets base diameter, spacing ratio, contrast, colors and viewing distance; every answer records the actual CSS-pixel diameter and gap. A twelve-answer check-in freezes the starting conditions. This implementation has not been clinically evaluated; the study below is background for the task family, not evidence of benefit from Mangekyo.

Crowded symbols is now implemented. Fine alignment, Gabor extensions and visual search remain proposals. Each should have its own task metrics, reversible settings and a short usability pilot before wider use.

## Defer binocular treatment features

Showing ordinary content with both eyes does not make it dichoptic training. A proposed binocular mode would need a method to deliver different images to each eye, calibration, an appropriate protocol and clinical input. A randomized trial of one home binocular game found no advantage over its placebo game; that result concerns the tested game, not every possible binocular intervention. [Gao et al., 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29302694/).

Do not add patching prescriptions, automatic eye-balance changes, red/green filter protocols, stereopsis scores, rapid flicker or eye-tracking claims to an ordinary phone screen on the basis of these ideas. Professional measurements remain separate from practice scores.
