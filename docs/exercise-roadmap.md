# Further exercise directions

Reviewed October 26, 2026. This is a product research note, not a treatment plan. Study protocols, displays, participants and supervision differ from Mangekyo. A related laboratory task does not validate this app or establish a benefit for a particular person. No training dose or patching schedule is recommended here.

## Now available: touch coordination

**Tap targets** practices visually guided pointing. It records hits, attempt latency, distance from the target center, target size and hit tolerance. A miss retains the target and earned points. The hit area never falls below 44 CSS pixels. Five-answer accuracy blocks adjust tolerance independently from scheduling.

**Follow the target** practices continuous cursor control. A handle 56 CSS pixels below the cursor keeps the finger away from the visual target. Dragging starts active timing; releasing, losing contact, pausing, interruption or geometry changes stop it. Recovery requires confirmation and a fresh grab. Reports contain time within the halo, time-weighted mean distance, and best continuous time on target. Ten seconds of actual tracking performance can adjust halo tolerance, while path speed stays at its chosen setting.

Adult research has documented reach/grasp coordination differences in amblyopia. That provides a reason to explore coordination tasks; it does not establish the treatment efficacy of these two phone activities. [Grant and Conway, 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38032338/).

| **Crowded symbols**                         | Identify a central symbol surrounded by other symbols. Begin with comfortable symbol size; vary the spacing independently. | Adult crowded-letter training improved the practiced spacing task and reduced measured crowding in a research study. This supports investigating the task family, without transferring the study's benefit claims to a phone adaptation. Record offsets in CSS pixels, not clinical acuity. Screen resolution and antialiasing impose a firm precision limit.                                             |
| **Gabor orientation / contrast comparison** | Compare the tilt or contrast of softly bounded stripe patches, with optional surrounding patches.                          | Structured low-level perceptual training has been studied in adults with amblyopia. Those results depended on a specific research protocol, rather than generic patterned stimulation. [Polat et al., 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15096608/).                                                     | Extend the existing discrimination framework before creating another near-duplicate exercise. | **Visual search**                           | Find a target symbol among similar distractors; adjust distractor count, spacing and similarity separately.                | An engagement and attention proposal here. Let precision lead; make speed pressure optional. Keep selection areas generous and separate search time from finger travel time where practical.                            |

These candidates have **not** been implemented in this release. The recommended next implementation is crowded symbols, followed by fine alignment. Each should have its own task metrics, reversible settings and a short usability pilot before wider use.

## Defer binocular treatment features

Showing ordinary content with both eyes does not make it dichoptic training. A proposed binocular mode would need a method to deliver different images to each eye, calibration, an appropriate protocol and clinical input. A randomized trial of one home binocular game found no advantage over its placebo game; that result concerns the tested game, not every possible binocular intervention. [Gao et al., 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29302694/).

Do not add patching prescriptions, automatic eye-balance changes, red/green filter protocols, stereopsis scores, rapid flicker or eye-tracking claims to an ordinary phone screen on the basis of these ideas. Professional measurements remain separate from practice scores.
